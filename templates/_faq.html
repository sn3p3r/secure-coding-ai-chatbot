FAQ

Where do I find SecureMentor?

Put your cursor on the bottom-right corner of the page. The SECUREMENTOR tab rises out of the corner (it also peeks up for a moment whenever a page loads). Click it to open the mentor. On a level, use its buttons for help with exactly where you are.

Is this website usable on a phone or iPad?

Not yet. Pages open on a phone, but playing needs a keyboard (WASD, Space, E, F, Q, 1-5), so use a laptop or desktop for the levels.

Will SecureMentor give me the answer?

No. It explains the idea behind a level and nudges you towards it. Any reply that contains the terminal's exact code is filtered out before it reaches you.

Who can see my progress, achievements and time?

You decide. Open PROFILE, then the SETTINGS & PRIVACY tab: choose who can open your profile, who sees your progress, achievements and time spent, whether you appear in friend search, and whether your times show on the leaderboard.

How do friends work?

Search a username on the FRIENDS page and send a request. You are friends once they add you back. Requests you received wait under REQUESTS; the ones you sent are under SENT.

I lost my items or my place in a level.

Passing a door saves a checkpoint on the server. Reload the level and choose RESUME AT CHECKPOINT. Inventories are kept per course, so items never carry over between courses.
